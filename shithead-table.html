Rules used on this site

Each player begins with 3 cards in hand, 3 face-up cards and 3 face-down cards. While the stock exists, draw back to 3 cards after playing.

  • Normal cards must be equal to or higher than the current effective top card.
  • 2 can be played on anything and resets the pile.
  • 7 makes the next play 7 or lower.
  • 8 is transparent: the card beneath it still controls what may be played.
  • 10 burns the pile and gives the same player another turn.
  • Four consecutive cards of the same rank also burn the pile.
  • If you cannot or do not want to play, pick up the whole pile.
  • After your hand and the stock are empty, use your face-up cards. Then play your face-down cards blindly.
  • If a blind card cannot legally be played, you take that card and the whole pile into your hand.
  • The first player with no cards left wins.
