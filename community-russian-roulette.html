How the game works

Schedule and sign-up

Games begin at :00, :20 and :40 each hour. Sign-up opens ten minutes before the start time. Choose a stake from 100 to 10,000 play chips.

At least two players are required. If the game does not fill, every entry is automatically refunded. A maximum of ten players may join.

Turns and queued moves

Turn order is randomised when the game starts. Each active player has one minute to choose Play or Leave. Players waiting for their turn can queue either action in advance.

A player who reaches the deadline without a queued or manual choice automatically leaves and banks their current cash-out value.

Chamber odds

The first pull has a 1 in 6 elimination chance. A safe pull removes an empty chamber, making the next chance 1 in 5, then 1 in 4, 1 in 3, 1 in 2 and finally 1 in 1. After an elimination, the cylinder resets to 1 in 6 for the remaining players.

Multipliers and payouts

Every safe pull increases that player's personal multiplier using the actual survival odds they faced. For example, surviving a 1 in 6 pull changes 1.00× to 1.20×. The potential cash-out is always rounded down to whole chips.

Leaving pays the current cash-out value. An elimination pays nothing. When only one active player remains, that player wins immediately and receives their cash-out plus a 25% survivor bonus.
