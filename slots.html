HOW IT WORKS

Rules and fairness

Ten paylines

A winning result displays five matching symbols across the centre payline and pays twice the total bet. A losing result pays nothing.

Server-authoritative spins

The database deducts the bet, generates the full grid, evaluates every line and credits the payout in one protected transaction. Changing the browser animation cannot change the result.

Same odds on every cabinet

Lucky Sevens, Neon Rush and Cosmic Vault use identical financial odds. Their symbols and visual presentation are the only differences.

Expected return

Half of spins return 2× the total bet and half return 0. The expected payout is exactly 1× the bet, producing a theoretical RTP of 100%.
