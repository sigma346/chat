HOW IT WORKS

Rules and fairness

Different risk profiles

Lucky Sevens pays on 50% of spins, Neon Rush on 35%, and Cosmic Vault on 19.5%. Lower-frequency cabinets compensate with larger possible prizes.

Emoji-specific prizes

Each winning emoji has its own probability and multiplier. A common Cherry may only return the bet, while the Cosmic Galaxy pays 30×.

Server-authoritative spins

Supabase locks the wallet, charges the bet, chooses the outcome, creates the displayed grid, credits the payout and stores the history in one transaction.

100% theoretical RTP

Each machine's probabilities and multipliers are balanced so its expected long-run payout equals the amount wagered. Short sessions can still vary dramatically, because probability enjoys theatre.
